How to play

  1. New here? Press Start here: the lesson first. It is a short step-by-step lesson with notes, a rocket lab, and practice.
  2. Each quiz round has 9 questions in 3 styles: Build it, Spot the mistake, and Predict the motion.
  3. In a free-body diagram, the dot is the object. Each arrow is a force acting on it. The arrow points the way the force pushes or pulls. A longer arrow means a stronger force.
  4. Right on the first try earns 100 points. Streaks earn bonus points.
